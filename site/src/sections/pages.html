Any page

DOM or canvas. Desktop or phone.

brokit doesn't care what made the page. If Chrome can show it, brokit can click it, read it and take its picture.

Plain HTML and every framework

Point at a file and brokit serves its folder, or point at your dev server.

brokit run index.html

Canvas, WebGL, WebGPU

No DOM to query? Click a spot inside the canvas and read the pixels it drew.

"from": "document.querySelector('canvas')"

Phones, light and dark

Screen size, pixel density, touch, taps, swipes and flicks, the notch, dark mode.

--size 393x852 --dpr 3 --mobile
