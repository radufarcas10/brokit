Free, open-source CLI and Node.js library

Check any web page in a real Chrome.

brokit opens your pages in a headless Chrome, uses them as a person would (clicks, typing, touch), checks what they show and compares screenshots. On your computer, in CI, or run by your AI agent.

Needs Node.js 22.4+ and Chrome, Chromium or Edge · no npm dependencies · MIT or Apache-2.0
